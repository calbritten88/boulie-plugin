# Boulie plugin

Boulie is a training platform your own AI operates. Your AI brings the coaching conversation. Boulie holds your goal, your plan and your coaching record, so every conversation picks up where the last one left off.

This plugin connects your AI to Boulie and adds three skills that help it work well:

- **boulie-get-started**: sets up a new athlete's coaching, from a first conversation to a saved goal, a season outline and a detailed current week.
- **boulie-coaching-session**: runs a check-in. Catches up on what happened, reviews completed sessions against the plan, answers your question and saves what you agreed.
- **boulie-plan-and-adjust**: plans or changes your training, from a single workout to a week or a block, including when life gets in the way.

Your AI always proposes changes and asks before saving anything to your plan.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin.
2. Connect Boulie when prompted (in Claude, from the plugin's Connectors tab) and sign in with the email you use for Boulie.
3. Ask: "Help me set up my coaching in Boulie."

The plugin uses Boulie's remote MCP server at `https://mcp.boulie.ai/mcp`. A Boulie account is required. Boulie provides training guidance, not medical advice.
